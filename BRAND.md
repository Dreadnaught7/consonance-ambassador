# Consonance visual identity

Identity hierarchy: **EJFinkley Holdings → Consonance → Compass**.

The master mark is an open circular system formed by two opposing curved signal/C forms around a stable central alignment point. It represents signal, bidirectionality, provenance, observation and alignment without forcing conformity.

Use the icon alone at small sizes; pair it with **CONSONANCE** as the primary wordmark and **EJFINKLEY HOLDINGS** as the provenance line. **Compass** is the product descriptor.

Foundation palette: near-black and warm white. Use restrained gold only for meaningful confirmed states such as verified referral provenance, approved commission or aligned/confirmed status. Avoid gradients, glow and generic AI imagery.

Brand character: institutional, intelligent, technological, human-centered, provenance-aware, minimal and calm.

Canonical themes: Intelligence • Alignment • Provenance • Humanity.

Where analytical reports are produced, close with an evidence-grounded **Summarizing Principle** that introduces no new facts and does not overstate certainty.
